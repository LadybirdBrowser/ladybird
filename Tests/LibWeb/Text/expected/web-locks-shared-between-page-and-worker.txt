== A worker cannot take a lock that its page holds
worker: held-by-page is not available
== query() in the worker lists the page's lock
worker: held [held-by-page (exclusive)], pending []
== query() in the page lists the worker's lock
worker: holds held-by-worker
page: held [held-by-worker (shared)]
== A request of the worker waits until the page releases the lock
page: pending [released-by-page (exclusive)]
page: releases released-by-page
worker: granted released-by-page
== The page steals a lock from the worker
worker: holds stolen-from-worker
page: stole stolen-from-worker
worker: the released promise of stolen-from-worker was rejected with AbortError
== The worker steals a lock from the page
page: the released promise was rejected with AbortError
worker: stole stolen-from-page
== Terminating the worker releases its lock
worker: holds held-by-terminated-worker and spins
page: granted held-by-terminated-worker
